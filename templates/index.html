02HOW IT WORKS

From camera
to category.

Each frame passes through a lightweight face-detection and classification loop. The result is a label, not a psychological assessment.

  1. 01

    Capture

    A frame is read from the computer’s webcam.

    ◎
  2. 02

    Find a face

    OpenCV’s Haar cascade locates faces in the grayscale frame.

    ⌗
  3. 03

    Prepare input

    Each detected face is cropped and resized to 48 × 48 pixels.

    ▦
  4. 04

    Classify

    The CNN scores seven expression classes; the highest score is shown.

    ↗
